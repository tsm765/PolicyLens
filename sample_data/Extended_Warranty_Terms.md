# Asteron Equipment — Extended Warranty Terms

**Document ID:** AST-EW-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose and relationship to standard coverage

These terms describe the optional extended warranty for the fictional Asteron Equipment AX-100 Standard Industrial Power Unit and AX-200 Heavy-Duty Industrial Power Unit. The extension follows the product's standard warranty; it does not replace, restart, or add time to the standard warranty period.

Standard coverage is governed by **AST-WAR-001, Product Warranty Policy**. For each product, standard coverage ends at the earlier of the commissioning-based deadline and the maximum shipment-based deadline specified in **AST-WAR-001 §3**. The extension begins immediately after that standard coverage ends, if the extension has been purchased and registered on time.

## 2. Eligible products and extension duration

### 2.1 Eligible products

An optional 12-month extension is available for both:

1. AX-100 Standard Industrial Power Unit.
2. AX-200 Heavy-Duty Industrial Power Unit.

The extension applies to the identified product unit for which the purchase and registration are recorded.

### 2.2 Duration

The extended coverage period is **12 months**. It follows the end of the applicable standard warranty period and covers the next 12 months. The extension does not change the AX-100 or AX-200 standard warranty duration or shipment maximum.

## 3. Purchase and registration timing

Both purchase and registration must be completed **before the standard warranty expires**. A purchase or registration made on or after the standard warranty expiry does not meet this timing requirement under these terms.

The applicable standard expiry must be determined under **AST-WAR-001 §3**, using the unit's shipment date, commissioning date, and product model. If those dates cannot be established, the standard expiry—and therefore whether the extension was obtained in time—cannot be confirmed until the relevant information is provided.

The purchase record and registration record should identify the same product model and serial number. If records are incomplete or inconsistent, the service team may request clarification through the service request process.

## 4. Start of extended coverage

When an extension has been purchased and registered before standard warranty expiry, extended coverage begins immediately after the standard warranty coverage ends and continues for 12 months. The extension follows the applicable standard period; it does not overlap that period or create an additional 12 months measured from the purchase date.

For example, if the standard warranty for a unit ends on its applicable expiry date, the extension follows that end point for the ensuing 12-month term. Buying the extension earlier does not cause the extended period to begin early. The standard warranty expiry remains subject to the earlier-deadline rule in **AST-WAR-001 §3.3**.

## 5. Extended coverage and exclusions

The extension covers the same type of fault as the standard warranty: a manufacturing defect under normal, authorized operation, subject to an eligibility review. The same exclusions apply. The extension does not cover damage caused by:

1. Improper handling.
2. Unauthorized modifications.
3. Failure to follow required maintenance instructions, where the damage results from that failure.

These terms extend the period in which the stated coverage may apply; they do not broaden the covered fault conditions or remove the exclusions in **AST-WAR-001 §4**. A final decision still requires sufficient information about the unit, dates, fault, and relevant records.

## 6. Service requests and repair authorization

Claims during extended coverage use the standard service request procedure in **AST-SVC-001, Service Procedures**. The reporter should provide the model, serial number, fault description, and relevant warranty records, including the extension purchase and registration details.

The service team reviews the request against both the applicable coverage terms and the facts provided. An extension registration does not by itself establish that a particular fault is covered. Warranty repair must be authorized by the service team before it begins, following **AST-SVC-001 §5**.

If information is incomplete, the service team may request clarification and escalate unresolved questions under **AST-SVC-001 §6**. A request for additional records is not an approval or denial of the claim.

## 7. Summary of the coverage sequence

1. Determine the product's standard warranty expiry using its model, shipment date, and commissioning date under **AST-WAR-001 §3**.
2. Confirm that the optional extension was both purchased and registered before that expiry.
3. If both timing requirements are met, extended coverage follows the end of standard coverage and lasts 12 months.
4. Apply the same manufacturing-defect coverage and exclusions during the extension.
5. Report and review a claim using **AST-SVC-001**; obtain authorization before warranty repair.
