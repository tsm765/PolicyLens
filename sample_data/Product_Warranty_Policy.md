# Asteron Equipment — Product Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** AST-WAR-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes the standard limited warranty for Asteron Equipment's AX-100 Standard Industrial Power Unit and AX-200 Heavy-Duty Industrial Power Unit. It explains the coverage period, the types of faults considered, and the information needed for an eligibility review.

This document describes the fictional Asteron Equipment policy used by the PolicyLens project. It is not a real manufacturer's warranty. The policy is a basis for review; it does not make a final eligibility decision without the relevant product, date, fault, and service information.

## 2. Definitions and date interpretation

1. **Shipment date** is the date the unit is recorded as shipped by Asteron Equipment.
2. **Commissioning date** is the date the unit is first placed into authorized operation and commissioning is recorded.
3. **Manufacturing defect** is a fault attributable to a defect in materials or workmanship when the unit is operated normally and in an authorized manner.
4. **Warranty expiry date** is determined using the applicable time limit in Section 3. Coverage ends at the earlier applicable deadline: the commissioning-based deadline or the maximum shipment-based deadline.
5. A period stated in months is measured by calendar months from the applicable start date. If a required date or record is unavailable, the expiry date cannot be confirmed from this policy alone.

## 3. Standard warranty periods

### 3.1 AX-100 — Standard Industrial Power Unit

The AX-100 standard warranty runs for **24 months from its commissioning date**, subject to a maximum of **30 months from its shipment date**. The warranty expires at whichever of those two applicable deadlines occurs first.

### 3.2 AX-200 — Heavy-Duty Industrial Power Unit

The AX-200 standard warranty runs for **18 months from its commissioning date**, subject to a maximum of **24 months from its shipment date**. The warranty expires at whichever of those two applicable deadlines occurs first.

### 3.3 Determining the applicable expiry

To identify the standard warranty expiry date, determine both deadlines for the correct model and compare them. The earlier deadline controls. The commissioning-based duration does not extend coverage beyond the maximum period measured from shipment. Likewise, the shipment maximum does not add time after the commissioning-based period has ended.

An eligibility review therefore needs a confirmed product model, shipment date, and commissioning date. If dates conflict or cannot be verified, the reviewer should request clarification under the service request process rather than assume a later expiry.

## 4. Coverage and exclusions

### 4.1 Covered faults

Subject to the applicable warranty period and an eligibility review, the standard warranty covers manufacturing defects that arise while the product is under normal, authorized operation. The nature of the reported fault and the available product and service records are considered during review.

### 4.2 Excluded damage

The standard warranty does not cover damage caused by:

1. Improper handling.
2. Unauthorized modifications.
3. Failure to follow required maintenance instructions, where the damage results from that failure.

The exclusion concerns damage caused by the listed circumstance. The existence of a circumstance alone does not establish that it caused a particular fault; the service review considers the fault description and relevant records.

## 5. Information needed for eligibility review

A final eligibility decision requires sufficient information to identify the unit, establish the relevant coverage dates, and understand the reported fault. This includes:

1. The product model and serial number.
2. The recorded shipment and commissioning dates, with available warranty records.
3. A description of the fault and when it was observed.
4. Relevant operating, handling, modification, and maintenance information when it may bear on the reported damage.

If key information is missing, the service team may ask for it before deciding whether the fault is eligible. A request for more information is not itself an approval or denial of coverage.

## 6. Relationship to service authorization

Warranty coverage and repair authorization are related but separate steps. A fault must be reviewed against this policy, and warranty repair must be authorized through the service procedure before that repair begins. See **AST-SVC-001, Service Procedures**, for reporting, service request, review, authorization, and escalation steps.

## 7. Policy boundaries

This policy states standard coverage durations, the shipment maximums, covered fault conditions, listed exclusions, and information needed for review. It does not itself determine whether a particular unit or fault qualifies when the necessary facts have not been provided. Where information is incomplete, use the service procedure to request records or escalate the open question.
